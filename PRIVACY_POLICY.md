# Privacy Policy: Guest Experience TV and Guest Experience Host

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

This policy covers two apps: **Guest Experience TV** (on the TV) and **Guest Experience Host** (on
the host's phone). Both are made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we").

## Summary

We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. The apps have no accounts, no ads, no
analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own devices.

## What the apps store, and where

The host may enter: the property name and area, a guest's first name, check-out date and time,
a welcome message, house notes, nearby places, host contact details, and the guest Wi-Fi network name
and password. The host may also choose a background photo on the TV.

- This information is stored **only on the TV** (and, when the Host app is used, on the host's phone).
- The **Wi-Fi password** is stored **encrypted** with a key held in the device's secure hardware
  keystore. The **Host PIN** is never stored. Only a salted one-way hash of it is kept.
- A chosen background photo is copied into the TV app's private storage and never leaves the TV.
- The apps turn off Android cloud backup, so this information is not copied to any cloud.

## Communication between the phone and the TV

When the host pairs the Host app with the TV, the two apps talk **directly over the property's local
Wi-Fi network**. Pairing needs a one-time code shown on the TV. After that, all messages are encrypted.
The information goes only from the host's phone to the host's TV. It is never sent to us or to any
server. The TV announces itself on the local network only while the app is open.

## Guests

Guests only view the screen. The apps do not collect any information from guests. Scanning the Wi-Fi
QR code is handled entirely by the guest's own phone.

## Deleting data

- **Clear for next guest** (Host Mode or the Host app) removes the guest's name, message and check-out.
- Uninstalling the app, or **Settings › Apps › Guest Experience TV › Clear storage**, deletes everything
  on that device, including the encryption key.

## Children

The apps are for property hosts and are not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.

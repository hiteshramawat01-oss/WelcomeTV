## Guest Experience TV

**Title** (≤ 30): `Guest Experience TV`

**Short description** (≤ 80):
`A personal welcome screen for your guests: Wi-Fi QR code, check-out time and tips.`

**Full description** (≤ 4000):

Give every guest a warm welcome the moment they turn on the TV.

Guest Experience TV turns the TV in your holiday rental, homestay or boutique hotel into an elegant
welcome screen:

• A personal greeting: "Good evening, welcome Ananya, to Pichola Haveli"
• Your guest Wi-Fi with a QR code: guests scan it with their phone camera and they're online. No more spelling out passwords.
• Check-out date and time, always visible
• House notes, nearby places and your contact details, shown one at a time
• A calm built-in Udaipur lake scene, or a photo of your own property
• A screensaver version of the welcome screen when the TV is idle (on TVs that allow it)

Built for hosts:
• Set up in under two minutes with just the remote
• Host Mode is protected by a PIN. Hold OK for 3 seconds to open it
• Update everything from your phone with the free Guest Experience Host app (same Wi-Fi)
• "Clear for next guest" in one click

Private by design:
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Everything stays on your TV. The Wi-Fi password is stored encrypted
• Guests can leave the app at any time with the Home button

Works on Android TV and Google TV.

**Category**: Travel & Local · **Tags**: hospitality, holiday rental, homestay, hotel, Wi-Fi QR

## Guest Experience Host

**Title**: `Guest Experience Host`

**Short description**:
`Update the welcome screen on your rental's TV from your phone: names, Wi-Fi, notes.`

**Full description**:

The companion app for Guest Experience TV.

Type on your phone instead of the TV remote. On the same Wi-Fi as your TV:
• Set the guest's name and check-out time before they arrive
• Change the Wi-Fi name and password shown in the QR code
• Edit house notes, nearby places and your contact details
• Switch the background and clock style
• "Next guest" clears the last stay in one tap

Pair once with the 6-digit code shown on the TV. After that, every change goes directly and encrypted
from your phone to your TV. Nothing is sent to us or to any server. No account needed.

Requires Guest Experience TV on an Android TV or Google TV on the same Wi-Fi network.

**Category**: Travel & Local

**Phone screenshots**: take on a real phone (Stay, Wi-Fi, Property tabs and the pairing screen).
